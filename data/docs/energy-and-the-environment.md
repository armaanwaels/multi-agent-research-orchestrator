# Energy and the environment explained

Source: https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/energy-and-the-environment/ (U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain)

## Energy has environmental impact

Producing, delivering, and using energy can affect the environment. The type and size of the effects vary.

## Overview of each major energy source's effects on the environment

* Nonrenewable energy
* Coal
* Natural gas
* Nuclear
* Oil and petroleum products
  + Gasoline
  + Diesel fuel

* Renewable energy
* Biomass
* Biofuels
* Geothermal
* Hydropower
* Solar
* Wind

* Secondary energy
* Electricity
